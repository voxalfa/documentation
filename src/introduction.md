# Introduction

Voxalfa is a declarative, line-based language for composing and storing [tonic sol-fa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonic_solfa) scores. It aims to keep scores compact and readable while remaining regular enough to be parsed, formatted, highlighted, and generated by tools.

A Voxalfa file can currently be converted to MIDI, SVG, and PDF; MusicXML support is planned.

## Highlights

- **Declarative:** a score describes what should be played, not how to draw it or how to schedule individual events.
- **Line-based and directive-driven:** declarations are written one line at a time and start with an explicit marker.
- **Typed:** metadata and parameters have well-defined types, so mistakes are reported by the compiler.
- **Composable and highly flexible:** sections, sub-sections, repeats, jumps, and timed dynamics make it possible to describe complete scores, and the language even supports per-voice lyrics.
- **Complete language toolchain:** a parser, formatter, validator, language server, and score renderers provide editor support, diagnostics, and output generation.
- **Text-first:** scores are plain text, so they are easy to version and diff, friendly to large language models, and can be generated from existing sheet music provided as images or PDFs.

## A minimal score

Below is a simple Voxalfa score illustrating how metadata, multi-part harmony, lyrics, and repeat endings are represented seamlessly. It also demonstrates Voxalfa’s CSS-like directive system for custom visual styling.

```voxalfa
;; @version 0.1.0-alpha

[#] title="Follow me"
[#] author="Daniel Batchellor"
[#] description="A sample song from the Tonic Sol-Fa music course for schools."
[#] verses={1}
[#] language="en"
[#] tags={"example","practice"}

[$] key={G} | time={4,4} | tempo={adagio} | voices={S,A}

;; @title-text margin-bottom={20}
;; @note-text font-size={16}
;; @lyrics-text font-size={12} | color="#333333"

---

[$] mark={S}

[S] |d   :t-1 !d   :r   |m   :f   !m   :r   ||

[1] Fol_ low, me   come Fol_ low  me,  and  @@

++

[A] |    :    !    :    |d   :t-1 !d   :r   ||

[1] ~@4                 We   will fol_ low  @@

--

[$] ending={1} | jump={DS}

[S] |d   :t-1 !d   :r   |m   :f   !m   :    ||

[1] we   will sing in   har_ mo_  ny.@2\    @@

++

[A] |m   :f   !m   :r   |d   :t-1 !d   :    ||

[1] we   will sing in   har_ mo_  ny.@2\    @@

--

[$] ending={2}

[S] |d   :t-1 !d   :r   |m   :f   !m   :-   ||

[1] we   will sing in   har_ mo_  ny.@2

++

[A] |m   :f   !m   :r   |d   :t-1 !d   :-   ||

[1] we   will sing in   har_ mo_  ny!@2
```

Voxalfa is still in early development. Syntax and features may change before the first stable release. It is open-source and available on [GitHub](https://voxalfa/voxalfa).
